For a parent or guardian

Share a little
movie magic.

Email a photo of the colouring sheet to sales@casablancapopcorn.com. Please attach the picture to your email.

Photograph the artwork on its own. Keep faces, names, school details and other identifying information out of the photo.

In your email, please confirm: “I am an adult with permission to share this artwork, and I agree that Casablanca Popcorn may display it in its public colouring gallery.”

We review every submission. Approved artwork is added by hand; emailing a picture does not publish it automatically. We will not display your email address or your message.
