For the grown-ups

What’s in the mix?

Our Halloween mix brings together Classic Cinema, White Cheddar and All Dressed.

Each bag is a mix of these three recipes. The proportions vary from bag to bag. Nutrition information is provided separately for each recipe; it does not describe a measured serving of the Halloween mix.

Halloween mix contains milk. The mix includes White Cheddar, which contains milk ingredients.

Nutrition information per 50 g of each individual flavour. The figures below are reproduced from our flavour labels. A Halloween bag weighs 22 g and contains a varying mix of the three flavours. These tables are not nutrition values for one Halloween bag.
