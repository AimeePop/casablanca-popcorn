Our popcorn boutique

Small batches.
Star quality.

Welcome to Casablanca Popcorn, a popcorn boutique with a love of movie magic and a personal touch.

Our commercial kitchen is located in Kitchener, where we pop in small, intimate batches using coconut and avocado oils. Our popcorn is gluten-free and made with care, one batch at a time.

In October 2026, we opened our booth in Peddler’s Village at St. Jacobs Farmers’ Market—a little corner of Casablanca where you can meet us and find your favourite flavour.

Our Ontario-grown kernels

Grown close to home.

We source our kernels from Uncle Bob’s Popcorn, a family-run Ontario grower that has been growing popcorn since 1985. They are also Canada’s only grower of specialty white hull-less popcorn—the light, delicate variety used for our White Cheddar.

Our bags

A thoughtful
finishing touch.

Good popcorn deserves thoughtfully chosen packaging. We use bags made with NatureFlex™ film, a cellulose-based material derived from renewable wood pulp.

NatureFlex™ film is certified compostable. For your finished bag, follow the disposal instructions supplied with the packaging and check what your local composting programme accepts.
